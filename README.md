# Project Kart

A Mario Kart-style racer in a single HTML file. Pick a kart in the garage, then race 3 laps against 7 CPUs.

**Computer:** arrow keys or WASD to drive, Shift or Space to drift, R to get back on track, Esc to pause, M to mute.

**Phone:** the kart accelerates by itself. Slide your left thumb to steer, and hold Drift through corners, then let go for a boost.

To play over home Wi-Fi without the live site, double-click `start.command` on the Mac and open the address it shows on your phone.
