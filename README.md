# Project Kart

**[Play now](https://itzfireway.github.io/project-kart/)**

A Mario Kart-style racer in a single HTML file. Pick a kart in the garage, choose 1, 2, 3, 5 or 7 laps, then race 7 CPUs.

**Lucky blocks:** drive through the spinning ? blocks to get a random item: Turbo, Triple turbo, Oil slick, Homing rocket, Shield, Superstar or Zap. Racers near the back get the stronger items. The CPUs grab blocks, use items, dodge oil and drift through corners for boosts, more often on Hard. You hold two items: the second waits behind the first and moves up once you use it. You can turn lucky blocks off in the garage.

**Ramps and tricks:** hit a blue ramp to fly. Press drift in the air to do a trick, and you get a short speed boost when you land.

**Shortcuts:** two dirt lanes cut corners, one inside the hairpin and one past the pond. Look for the green SHORTCUT signs. CPUs take them too, more often on Hard.

**Computer:** arrow keys or WASD to drive, Shift or Space to drift, E to use your item, R to get back on track, Esc to pause, M to mute.

**Phone:** the kart accelerates by itself. Slide your left thumb to steer, and hold Drift through corners, then let go for a boost. Tap Item to use what you picked up. Sideways works best. On Android the race goes full screen and the phone buzzes when you get hit or boost.

**Race friends:** press Create room in the garage, send the invite link, and press Start race when everyone is in. Up to 8 players, with CPUs filling any empty spots if you like. Keep the host's game open on screen until the race ends.

To play over home Wi-Fi without the live site, double-click `start.command` on the Mac and open the address it shows on your phone.
